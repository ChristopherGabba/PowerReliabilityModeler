Purpose and engineering limits

The application is a diagram and data-model editor. It records equipment, connections, metadata, and manually configured failovers, and exports them as JSON. It does not perform load flow, short-circuit studies, protection coordination, or failover simulation. Displayed ratings and calculated MVA depend on values entered by the user.

You are responsible for reviewing your model and exported data before using them in engineering work. A diagram or export does not certify an electrical design or establish safe operating conditions.

Saving and sharing

Autosave depends on browser storage, network connectivity, and service availability. Keep exported copies of important models. Review JSON files before sharing them; exports can contain all model metadata and are readable by their recipients.
