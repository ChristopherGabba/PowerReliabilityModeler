# Electrical one-line models

The editor models equipment and its connections in an electrical distribution system.

## Language

**Indoor drawout breaker** (`indoor_drawout_breaker`):
A circuit breaker installed indoors with a withdrawable assembly.
_Avoid_: HV breaker (`hv_breaker`).

**Outdoor MV/HV breaker** (`outdoor_mv_hv_breaker`):
An outdoor circuit breaker for medium- or high-voltage systems.
_Avoid_: Outdoor breaker (`outdoor_breaker`), outdoor MV breaker (`outdoor_mv_breaker`).
