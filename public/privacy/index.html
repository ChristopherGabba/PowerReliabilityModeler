PowerSystemsModelToJSON

Privacy Policy

Effective September 21, 2026

PowerSystemsModelToJSON is operated by Christopher Gabba. This page explains how the application handles account information and electrical models.

Account information and Google sign-in

Clerk provides authentication. Email sign-in uses your email address and a verification code. If you choose Google, Google shares your account identifier, email address, and basic profile information, such as your name and profile image, with Clerk. This information is used to create and authenticate your account, display account details, and associate private projects with you.

The Google connection requests only basic sign-in scopes: openid, email, and profile. It does not request access to Gmail messages, Google Drive files, contacts, or calendars. Google account data is used for account authentication and management, not advertising.

Models and local storage

The application stores the projects you create or import, including equipment metadata, connections, failovers, diagram positions, and view settings. Completed edits are saved in your browser and synchronized to Cloudflare. Browser storage supports autosave and recovery during outages; authentication also uses browser cookies and storage.

Projects are associated with your account and are not publicly listed. Export downloads a JSON file to your device. Sharing that file is an action you control, and anyone who receives it can read its contents.

Service providers

Clerk processes account and authentication information. Cloudflare hosts the application and stores project data. These services also process technical request and security information needed to operate the service, such as network and device information. Their practices are described in the Clerk Privacy Policy and Cloudflare Privacy Policy. Google's processing is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Managing your data

You can edit and export projects, delete projects from your workspace, and manage account details through the account menu. Project deletion does not erase JSON files you have downloaded or shared. Some project metadata is retained to prevent deleted projects from being recreated by pending save requests. Clearing this site's browser data removes local copies from that browser.

For account deletion, stored-data deletion, or privacy questions, contact christopher.gabba@gmail.com. Requests are handled through that address; there is no automatic account-to-project deletion workflow.
